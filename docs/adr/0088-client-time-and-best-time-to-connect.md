# ADR 0088: Client time and the best time to connect

Status: accepted (2026-11-26)

## Context

Sales people in India reach clients in every time zone. Kunal asked that every company and employee page show the
client's local time next to his own (IST by default), whether now is a good moment to reach them and why, their working
hours converted to his time, and the country facts sales teams use: ISO alpha-2 and alpha-3 codes, calling code,
region (APAC, MENA, North America, South America...), and the currency's name, code and symbol. All of it must fill
itself in, including for companies that already exist, and he wants to be asked "are you sure?" before every email or
call, without ever being blocked.

## Decision

- **Worked out on read, from the address.** `packages/types/src/client-time.ts` resolves the IANA zone from the
  record's saved zone, else the state, else a city of 100,000+ people, else the headquarters text, else the country's
  main zone (marked as a guess when the country has several zones). Nothing is stored, so existing companies show
  everything as soon as their country is known, and a changed address is reflected at once.
- **Offline data, no AI.** `packages/types/src/geo-zones.ts` is generated by `scripts/geo/build-zone-data.mjs` from the
  dr5hn countries/states data (ODbL) and the GeoNames city list already shipped (ADR 0060): alpha-3 codes, UN
  subregions, state zones and city zones, about 90 KB. Currency names and symbols come from the platform's Intl data.
  Daylight saving comes from the IANA rules in Intl, never from a fixed offset.
- **Working hours.** `Company.workingHours` and `Contact.workingHours` hold `{ days, start, end, source }` on the
  client's clock. Auto-fill reads them from the company's schema.org `openingHoursSpecification`/`openingHours`
  (source `website`); typed hours are `user`; without either the card shows the country's usual day (09:00-18:00
  outside its weekend, Friday-Saturday in Saudi Arabia and others) and says it is an estimate.
- **Contact location wins.** An employee with their own country uses their own zone and hours; otherwise their
  company's. A company's further offices can be picked on the card.
- **Public holidays** come from the open `date-holidays` data (ISC code, CC BY-SA 4.0 data), public holidays only, at
  `GET /geo/countries/:code/holidays`. Countries it does not cover say "not checked".
- **My own hours** are per person (`UserContactTimes`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /me/contact-times`): zone (IST by default),
  calling hours (09:00-23:00) and "ask before I email or call" (on by default).
- **The check never blocks.** `ConnectCheck` wraps the company and employee pages and catches every `mailto:`/`tel:`
  link and any element marked `data-connect`, shows both clocks and the advice, and goes ahead on "Yes". It can be
  turned off from the dialog or Settings.

## Consequences

- Statuses: perfect timing, ending soon (last hour), starting soon (hour before), outside hours, weekend, public
  holiday; each with a sentence saying why, and a note when it falls outside my own calling hours.
- A person's own individual leave or local office closures are not known.
- Emails sent from sequences and automations are not checked; the check is for a person reaching out by hand.
